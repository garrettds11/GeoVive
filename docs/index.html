🗺️Explore the map

Everything starts here. The map works on any screen; on a phone, the sidebar becomes a tray you open with the ☰ bar.

✋ Move around

Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

👆 Inspect a place

Click a point to see its name, type and notes.

✈️ Fly to a place

Open Features and click an item; the map swoops in.

🎨 Change the look

Map Style: Streets, Outdoors, Light, Dark or Satellite.

🏷️ Filter by type

Tick categories in the legend to show or hide them.

📏 Measure

Scale bars (bottom-left) show miles and kilometers.

➤ Find yourself

The arrow button (top-left) centers on you. Your location stays on your device.

🧭 Compass, 🔒 lock & 3D

Reset north, lock north-up, or tilt into 3D terrain.

Datasets

A dataset is a collection of places. Choose one under Dataset. GeoVivé opens with World Capitals from Natural Earth; once you sign in, your own maps join the list.

🧅Layers

Stack more on the map from Layers in the sidebar. Your choices are remembered in this browser.

➕ More datasets

Show other datasets' points alongside the one you're browsing.

🏔️ Overlays

Live maps from public agencies: USGS topo, relief and imagery, streams and lakes, BLM land ownership, National Forests, state and county lines.

💡

Overlays cover the United States and come straight from each agency. If one is slow, that layer stays blank while everything else keeps working. Land ownership has detail down to about zoom 14.

🔌 Layers from connected apps

Apps and businesses that work with GeoVivé can bring their own layers, like hunting units, service areas or project sites. Arrive from one of those apps and its layers appear at the top of Layers in the AppConnect section, for that visit only. Click a shape for details, or Zoom to layer to jump there.

⚠️

These layers belong to the app that provides them. Shapes are simplified for display, so check the provider's official sources for exact boundaries.

🔑Signing in

Browsing needs no account. To make maps, click Sign in and pick either way in:

🟢 Google

Your account is created the first time. Google shows its account chooser each time, so switching is easy.

✉️ Email & password

Choose Create an account, then enter the code we email you. Forgot your password? Use the reset link.

🔗

One email, one account. Signed up with email and later chose Google with the same verified address? Both open the same maps. (The first time, you may need to click Sign in with Google twice.) If you started with Google, keep using Google.

Sign out signs you out of GeoVivé only, not Google. On a shared computer, sign out of Google too. Sessions last about a day.

🗂️My maps

Your collections live in My maps, each with its pin count.

  1. Type a name in New map name.
  2. Tick Public if anyone should be able to view it. Maps are private until you do.
  3. Click Create. Your empty map opens, ready for pins.
🗑️

Delete map removes the map and every pin in it. It can't be undone.

📍Pins

Open one of your maps and the edit bar appears.

  1. Click 📍 Add pin. The cursor turns into a crosshair.
  2. Click the spot on the map.
  3. Give it a Name, pick a Type and add any Notes.
  4. Click Save. Done!
🥾

Out in the field? 📍 Pin my location opens a new pin right where you're standing. Your browser asks first, and nothing is saved unless you save the pin.

✨ Make pins your own

📝 Markdown notes

**bold**, *italic*, - lists and links all display nicely.

😀 Emoji

Use the 😀 button, or Win + . on Windows, Ctrl + Cmd + Space on a Mac.

🔣 Symbol codes

Type U+1F3E0 and it shows as 🏠.

📋 Copy & share

Each pin card copies the pin as Markdown or as JSON.

To change or remove a pin, click it, then the ✏️ pencil. Press Esc to cancel adding a pin.

📥Importing data

Bring places in from elsewhere: open one of your maps and click Import data.

📄 GeoJSON fileA .geojson or .json file from your computer, up to 50 MB.
🔗 GeoJSON linkA public web address, such as an open-data download.
🏛️ ArcGIS layerAn address ending in FeatureServer/0 or MapServer/2, with an optional filter like STATE = 'CO'.

Under Options, choose which fields hold names and categories, and whether to add to the map or replace it. Imports run in the background (up to 20,000 features), and GeoVivé remembers where the data came from.

📤Exporting a map

Take your map anywhere. Under Dataset, pick a format and click Export.

🧩 GeoJSONFor GIS software and map apps. Includes where imported data came from.
🌍 KMLFor Google Earth and most mapping apps.
🛰️ GPXFor GPS units and outdoor apps. Pins become waypoints; lines and area edges become tracks.

You can export public maps and your own maps, at full detail.

🔒Public & private

🔒 Private

Only you can see it, even if someone has the link. This is the default.

🌐 Public

Anyone can view it, signed in or not. Only you can edit it.

💬FAQ

How do I switch Google accounts?

Sign out, click Sign in, and pick another account from Google's chooser (or Use another account).

I can't see the edit buttons.

Editing appears only on maps you own, while you're signed in. Other people's public maps are view-only.

Where does GeoVivé's data come from?

Public datasets name their source; World Capitals, for example, comes from Natural Earth (public domain). AppConnect layers come from the connected app and show its credits. Your maps contain only what you add.

Is my location shared?

No. The location button and Pin my location use your position only in your browser. It's saved only if you save a pin there.

Can my business bring its own layers?

Yes, with AppConnect. You keep your data; GeoVivé shows it to your users. Register on the AppConnect page.

How do I report a problem?

Email admin@geovive.link. See Trust & Security for how we protect your data.
